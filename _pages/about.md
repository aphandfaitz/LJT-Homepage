I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://hkust.edu.hk/), working with [Prof. Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/). I received my B.Eng. from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/) in June 2024.

## Research Interests

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. In particular, I am interested in:
- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**: Learning verifiable reasoning capabilities in large language models.
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**: Understanding the perception bottleneck of VLMs for chart and image understanding.
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**: Investigating the internal representations of truthfulness in LLMs and building scalable verification mechanisms.
